Welcome Guide & Getting Started

Welcome to BizTrace IMS

Dear Company Admin, Welcome to BizTrace IMS, your complete business management system for managing sales, purchases, inventory, expenses, customers, suppliers, payments, reports, and other business operations from one place. Thank you for registering your company with us. Below is a quick guide to help you get started and get the best experience from BizTrace IMS.

01

Company Setup & Configuration

  • Company name and business information
  • Currency and currency symbol
  • Timezone
  • Date and time format
  • Invoice numbering
  • Purchase numbering
  • Stock settings
  • Low-stock notifications
  • Other business preferences
Recommended device: Use a PC/Desktop/Tablet for the best experience when configuring settings. Mobile users should keep their phone in Landscape mode for settings and areas containing tables, forms, multiple fields and detailed information.
02

Users & Staff

Company Admin can create staff accounts and assign roles and permissions for different parts of the system.

  • Dashboard
  • Products
  • Sales
  • Purchases
  • Customers
  • Suppliers
  • Expenses
  • Reports
  • Company Settings
Best practice: Only give staff members the permissions required for their responsibilities. Each staff member should have their own individual account.
03

Products & Inventory

Add and maintain accurate product information.

  • Product name
  • SKU
  • Category
  • Unit
  • Purchase price
  • Selling price
  • Opening quantity
  • Minimum stock level

Monitor current quantity, stock value, selling value, low-stock items, out-of-stock products and potential profit.

Enter opening quantities correctly before recording regular transactions.
04

Purchases

Use Purchases to record inventory acquired from suppliers.

  • Supplier information
  • Products
  • Quantities
  • Purchase prices
  • Payment information
  • Outstanding balances

Keeping purchase records updated helps maintain accurate inventory and supplier information.

05

Sales

Record customer sales accurately and promptly.

  • Customer sales
  • Products and quantities
  • Discounts
  • Payments
  • Paid invoices
  • Partially paid invoices
  • Unpaid invoices
  • Customer balances
Record sales promptly and accurately to keep inventory, payment and financial records current.
06

Expenses

Record business expenses such as:

  • Transportation
  • Rent
  • Utilities
  • Salaries
  • Maintenance
  • Office expenses
  • Operational expenses
  • Other business expenses
Select the correct payment method, especially when recording cash expenses. Cash expenses are important for cash management and deposit calculations.
07

Cash & Payments

BizTrace IMS supports payment methods such as:

  • Cash
  • Bank Transfer
  • Card
  • Other
Cash Available Calculation

Cash Received − Cash Expenses − Cash Deposited = Cash Available

Record cash sales, expenses and deposits accurately so that cash information remains reliable.

08

Reports

BizTrace IMS provides business information through reports including:

  • Sales reports
  • Purchase reports
  • Expense reports
  • Inventory reports
  • Profitability reports
  • Receivables
  • Payables
  • Payment information
  • Stock valuation
  • Cash information
  • Other business summaries

Use the available date filters to review information for the required period.

Important Report Printing Guidelines
  • Strongly advisable to use a PC/Desktop.
  • Switch BizTrace IMS to Light Mode.
  • Confirm that the report displays correctly.
  • Click Print.
  • Review the print preview.
  • If a chart or image is missing, cancel the print preview and select Print again.
  • You may need to repeat the process until the chart or image appears.
  • Always check the final print preview before printing.
09

Mobile & Device Usage

  • PC/Desktop: Recommended for full system usage, configuration and printing.
  • Tablet: Suitable for most operations and configuration.
  • Mobile: Suitable for quick access and routine operations.
  • Mobile devices can also be used for sales invoice printing.
  • Use Landscape mode for settings, tables, reports, invoices, products and multi-field forms.
  • PC/Desktop is recommended for report printing.
10

Security

  • Keep passwords confidential.
  • Never share the administrator login.
  • Use individual staff accounts.
  • Assign permissions carefully.
  • Change passwords when necessary.
  • Log out when using shared computers.
  • Avoid saving passwords on public or shared devices.

  • Use a PC/Desktop whenever possible.
  • Switch to Light Mode before printing.

  • Company name
  • Registered email

17

Best Practices

  • Complete company settings before regular transactions.
  • Add products and opening stock correctly.
  • Give staff individual accounts.
  • Assign only required permissions.
  • Record purchases promptly.
  • Record sales promptly.
  • Record expenses accurately.
  • Select the correct payment method.
  • Record cash expenses and deposits accurately.
  • Review reports regularly.
  • Keep login credentials secure.
  • Use Light Mode when printing.
  • Use PC/Desktop for printing and major configuration.
  • Use mobile/tablet devices for routine operations and sales invoice printing.
  • Use Landscape mode for detailed pages.
  • Keep your subscription active always to avoid ₦500 maintenance fee per day after one month of not subscribing.
Following these practices will help keep your BizTrace IMS records accurate, organized and easier to manage.
