Dear Company Admin,

Thank you for registering your company with BizTrace IMS. This guide explains the main features of the system and provides recommendations for configuring and using your company workspace.

Getting started

We recommend configuring your company settings first, then creating users, adding products and setting up your opening stock before recording regular transactions.

1. Company Setup & Configuration

Configure your company workspace before starting regular operations.

After registration, the Company Admin should first review the company settings and configure:

  • Company name and business information
  • Company logo
  • Currency and currency symbol
  • Timezone
  • Date and time format
  • Invoice numbering
  • Purchase numbering
  • Booking numbering
  • Stock settings
  • Low-stock notifications
  • Other business preferences
Recommended device

For the best experience when configuring company settings, use a PC, Desktop or Tablet where possible. If using a mobile phone, keep the phone in Landscape mode, especially when working with tables, forms or multiple fields.

👥

2. Users & Staff

Manage staff access and permissions.

The Company Admin can create staff accounts and assign appropriate roles and permissions.

You can control who can:

  • View the dashboard
  • Manage products
  • Manage sales
  • Manage purchases
  • Manage customers
  • Manage suppliers
  • Record expenses
  • Manage bookings
  • View reports
  • Manage company settings
Security recommendation

Only give users the permissions required for their responsibilities. Each staff member should have an individual account instead of sharing login credentials.

  • Product name

  • Selling price

BizTrace IMS can help you monitor:

Always ensure product information and opening quantities are entered correctly before recording transactions.

10. Security

Protect your company account and data.

  • Keep passwords confidential.
  • Never share administrator login details.
  • Create individual accounts for staff.
  • Assign permissions carefully.
  • Change passwords when necessary.
  • Log out from shared computers.
  • Avoid saving passwords on public or shared devices.
Company Admin responsibility

The Company Admin is responsible for properly managing company users, permissions and account security.

14. Account & Company Deactivation

What happens when a company account is deactivated.

If your company account is deactivated, you may be unable to access the BizTrace IMS dashboard and company features.

Deactivation may occur when:

  • A subscription expires.
  • Payment has not been confirmed.
  • The company account requires administrative attention.
  • The account has been manually deactivated by the System Administrator.

15. Printing & Display Guidelines

Important instructions for printing reports and documents.

PC/Desktop recommended

For the best printing experience, it is strongly advisable to use a PC or Desktop computer.

Before printing any report:

1
Open the report you want to print.
2
Switch BizTrace IMS to Light Mode.
3
Confirm that the report is displayed correctly.
4
Click the Print option.
5
Review the print preview before sending the document to the printer.
Charts and images may require repeated printing

Some reports may contain charts, images or other graphical elements. In some cases, a chart or image may not appear immediately in the browser's print preview. If this happens, cancel the print preview and select Print again. You may need to repeat the print action until the chart or image appears correctly.

Always print in Light Mode

For reports, invoices and other documents, always switch BizTrace IMS to Light Mode before printing for better readability and print output.

Before sending a document to the printer, always check the print preview and confirm that all pages, information, charts and images are displayed correctly.
