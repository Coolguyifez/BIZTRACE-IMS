Welcome to BizTrace IMS

Your complete business management system for managing sales, purchases, inventory, expenses, customers, suppliers, payments, reports, users, roles and everyday business operations from one place.

01

Company Setup & Configuration

Configure your business before starting daily operations.

Set up your company

After registration, the Company Admin should configure the company information and business preferences before inviting staff or recording transactions.

  • Set your currency and currency symbol.
  • Configure the timezone.
  • Select your preferred date format and time format.
  • Select the day on which your business week starts.
  • Configure invoice, purchase and expense numbering.
  • Configure number length, date inclusion and number reset preferences.
  • Configure stock and low-stock notification preferences.
  • Configure other business preferences available in Settings.

    Note: Financial category in Notification preference gives low-stock, gross-loss & net-loss notifications.

Recommended setup order

Configure company information first, then currency, timezone, numbering, inventory preferences, notifications, users, roles and permissions before beginning regular transactions.

02

Users & Staff

Create staff accounts and control what each user can access.

Managing users

Company Admins can create and manage staff accounts. Each staff member should have an individual account rather than sharing login credentials.

  • Create a staff account with the required personal and login information.
  • Assign one or more appropriate roles to the user.
  • Give users only the permissions required for their responsibilities.
  • Review user access whenever responsibilities change.
  • Disable or remove access when a staff member should no longer use the system.
03

Roles & Permissions

Control what users can see and what actions they can perform in BizTrace IMS.

How roles work

A role is a collection of permissions assigned to a user. Instead of assigning every permission individually to every staff member, you can create a role that represents a person's responsibilities and assign that role to the user.

BizTrace IMS provides default roles that can be used as a starting point. Your Company Admin can also create a custom role when the default roles do not match your business structure.

Default Roles

The following roles provide a practical starting structure for common business responsibilities. The exact permissions available to a role can be adjusted according to your application's permission configurations.

Note: Whatever permission is missing in a default role will be re-registered when system updates.

Creating a New Custom Role

If you do not like the default roles or your business has a different organizational structure, you can create a new role and select the exact permissions required for that role.

1

Open Roles

Go to the Users/Roles or Roles section available to your Company Admin.

2

Create Role

Select the option to create a new role and enter a clear role name.

3

Add Description

Add a description explaining what the role is intended to do.

4

Select Permissions

Select the permissions the new role should have. You can choose only the permissions required for the job.

5

Save the Role

Save the role after reviewing the selected permissions.

6

Assign the Role

Open the staff user's account and assign the newly created role.

Permission Groups

When creating a custom role, choose permissions according to the responsibilities of the user. Avoid giving administrative permissions to users who do not require them.

Use the principle of least privilege

Give a user only the permissions necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities. For example, a sales staff member may need to create sales and view products without needing access to company settings, roles or user management.

04

Products & Inventory

Maintain accurate product and stock information.

Managing inventory

  • Create and maintain product records.
  • Install Store keep Unit (SKU) code e.g BT-001, on all your products for easy identification.
  • Specify product category, e.g Electronics and Product Unit e.g Item, 1kg, 100g, piece, etc.
  • Enter relevant product information and pricing.
  • Enter minimum stock (that is when it reaches low quantity) and opening stock (if you have previous quantities).
  • Monitor available stock.
  • Review low-stock notifications.
  • Keep stock information updated after purchases and sales.
  • Review inventory information regularly to identify discrepancies.
  • Adjust, view, and retain product stock when needed.
05

Purchases

Record goods and products purchased for your business.

Purchase management

Purchases allow the business to record products obtained from suppliers and maintain accurate financial and inventory records.

  • Select the supplier.
  • Select the order (purchase) date and expected date.
  • Select the purchased products.
  • Enter quantities and product section will take care of the pricing.
  • Apply discounts or taxes (e.g transportation etc.) where applicable.
  • Record payment information and a note to write the account details of the supplier you paid to.
  • Review the purchase before completing it.
  • you can edit if you have outstanding balances (payables) or missed a quatity.
06

Sales

Record customer transactions and track sales activity.

Sales management

  • Select an existing customer or enter the required customer information.
  • Add one or multiple products to a sale.
  • Enter the required quantities.
  • Apply discounts or taxes (POS, bank transfer charges, etc.) where applicable.
  • Select the payment method, either cash, POS, bank transfer, online Card, or other forms of payment.
  • Record the bank paid into, payment reference, and information where applicable.
  • Review and complete the transaction.
  • Print or access the generated invoice when required.
07

Expenses

Record and monitor business expenses.

Expense management

Record business expenses so that financial activities can be tracked and included in relevant reports.

  • Enter the expense description.
  • Select the appropriate expense category.
  • Enter the amount.
  • Record payment information where applicable.
  • Review expense records regularly.
08

Cash & Payments

Keep payment information connected to your business transactions.

Sales Payment tracking

  • Record payment status for transactions.
  • Track paid, partially paid and unpaid amounts where supported.
  • Record relevant bank information.
  • Record payment transaction or bank references.
  • Record cash deposit transactions and bank references.
  • Review payment information before completing financial records.
09

Reports

Use business reports to review recorded activities.

Business reporting

Reports help authorized users review information recorded in BizTrace IMS and monitor business activities.

  • Review sales activity.
  • Review purchase activity.
  • Review expenses.
  • Review inventory information.
  • Review payment information.
  • Review outstanding balances made to suppliers (Payables).
  • Review outstanding balances made by customers (Receivables).
  • Review profit & loss information.
  • Review cash deposit information.
  • Review customer activities.
  • Review staff activities.
  • Export & review reports where the relevant permission is available.

Mobile & Device Usage

Use BizTrace IMS comfortably across supported devices.

PC / Desktop

Desktop computers provide the most convenient experience for settings, tables, forms and detailed business management.

Tablet

Tablets can be used for most daily activities and provide a larger interface for forms and tables.

Mobile

On mobile devices, landscape orientation is recommended for Settings, tables, forms and the Welcome Guide.

Allow each page to finish loading

When using BizTrace IMS, click an action once and allow the page to load before clicking again. This helps prevent duplicate submissions.

11

Security

Protect your company account and business information.

Account security

  • Never share your password with another user.
  • Give each staff member an individual account.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Assign only the permissions required for a user's responsibilities.
  • Review user accounts and roles periodically.
  • Disable accounts that should no longer have access.
12

Subscription

Manage your BizTrace IMS subscription and access.

Subscription management

Your subscription determines the access and availability of the features provided by your BizTrace IMS plan.

  • Review your current subscription information.
  • Monitor subscription status and expiry information.
  • Renew your subscription when required.
  • Keep payment and company information up to date.
13

Payment Details

Follow the payment instructions provided by the BizTrace IMS administrator.

Subscription payment

When a subscription payment is required, use the official payment information displayed by BizTrace IMS or provided by the system administrator.

After making a payment, retain the transaction evidence and follow the application's instructions for submitting payment information where applicable.

14

Subscription Renewal

Keep your company account active by monitoring subscription expiry.

Renewal process

  1. Check your subscription status.
  2. Review the displayed renewal information.
  3. Make payment using the official payment instructions.
  4. Submit the payment receipt with the company name and email to the appropriate platforms.
  5. Wait for the subscription status to be updated before attempting repeated payments. You can check that on the official platforms and website (click on this).
  6. Make sure to renew on time. Failure to renew the subscription after one month of expiration will result in an extra maintenance fee per day. Fees held will be sent to you through our channels.
15

Account & Company Deactivation

Understand what happens when access is disabled.

Deactivation

Company or user access may be disabled by an authorized administrator according to the system's account and subscription rules.

  • A disabled user should no longer use the account.
  • Company administrators should review staff access regularly.
  • Subscription-related restrictions should be resolved through the appropriate renewal or administrative process. Failure to resolve the subscription after two months of expiration will result in the company and user's account being lost or taken down.
16

General Printing & Display Tips

Get cleaner results when working with invoices, reports and the Welcome Guide.

Printing

  • Use a desktop or tablet where possible for printing tasks.
  • Use mobile phones or POS when printing sales invoice.
  • Make sure you are on light mode and check the page preview before printing.
  • Use landscape orientation for wide tables.
  • Ensure the correct printer is selected.
  • Use the browser's print preview to adjust margins and scaling where necessary.
17

Need Help?

Get assistance when something does not work as expected.

Before requesting support

  • Confirm that you are logged into the correct account.
  • Check that your user has the required permission.
  • Refresh the page if information appears stale.
  • Check your internet connection when using the online version.
  • Make sure you clicked the required action only once and allowed the page to finish loading.
  • Contact the system administrator if the issue concerns your account, role or permissions.
18

Best Practices

A few habits can help keep your BizTrace IMS data organized.

Use Individual Accounts

Do not share accounts between staff members. Give each person their own account and role.

Control Permissions

Assign permissions based on each person's responsibilities rather than giving everyone administrative access.

Review Regularly

Periodically review users, roles, products, transactions, payments and reports.

Enable browser notifications

Every user, including administrators, should visit the Notifications section and enable browser notifications when they want to receive outward notifications from BizTrace IMS.
