Inventory

Configure how BizTrace handles stock and inventory operations.

Inventory controls

These settings apply company-wide. Individual product minimum stock levels remain configurable on each product.

Allow negative stock

Allow inventory quantities to fall below zero when completing stock-consuming transactions.

Reserve stock

Reserve available inventory when a transaction requires stock to be held before completion.

Low-stock notifications

Notify assigned users when a product reaches or falls below its configured minimum stock level.

Require adjustment note

Require users to provide a reason when manually adjusting inventory quantities.

Sound Preferences

Control which notification sounds are played for your account.

Sounds do not control notification delivery

These settings only determine whether a sound is played when you receive a notification. Notification delivery is controlled separately by the notification assignments.

Success

Play a sound for successful operations.

System

Play a sound for system events.

Errors

Play a sound when an operation fails.

Net Loss

Play a sound for net-loss financial alerts.

Gross Loss

Play a sound for gross-loss financial alerts.

Low Stock

Play a sound when assigned low-stock alerts arrive.

Example: a user can have the Sales notification assigned while Success sound is disabled. The user still receives the Sales notification, but no success sound is played.
Changes apply to company settings, notification assignments, and your personal sound preferences.
Reset settings?

This will restore the company settings to the BizTrace default values. Existing documents, transactions, notification assignments, and personal notification sound preferences will not be changed.
