How reservations work
Reserving stock does not reduce the physical quantity. It increases the reserved quantity and reduces the quantity available for new sales.
Releasing a reservation decreases the reserved quantity and makes those units available again.

Reserved Stock
Stock held for a purpose

Reserved units remain physically in your inventory but cannot be treated as available stock for another transaction.

Available Stock
Stock available for use

Available stock is calculated as physical stock minus reserved stock.
